The Starry Night

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year of Creation: 1889

"The Starry Night" is an oil on canvas painting by Dutch Post-Impressionist painter Vincent van Gogh. Painted during his stay at the asylum of Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, it depicts the view from his east-facing bedroom window just before sunrise, with the addition of an idealized village. It is widely regarded as his magnum opus.
