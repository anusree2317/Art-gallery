The Persistence of Memory

Artist: Salvador Dalí

Year of Creation: 1931

"The Persistence of Memory" is a 1931 painting by Spanish artist Salvador Dalí. One of the most recognizable works of Surrealism, the painting depicts a surrealist landscape with melting pocket watches draped over various objects. It is often interpreted as a commentary on the abstract nature of time.
