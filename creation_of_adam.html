The Creation of Adam

Artist: Michelangelo

Year of Creation: c. 1508–1512

"The Creation of Adam" is a fresco painting by Italian artist Michelangelo, which forms part of the Sistine Chapel's ceiling. The scene illustrates the Biblical story from the Book of Genesis in which God gives life to Adam, the first man. The fresco is one of the most iconic images in the history of art, particularly for the almost-touching hands of God and Adam.
