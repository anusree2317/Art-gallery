Girl with a Pearl Earring

Artist: Johannes Vermeer

Year of Creation: c. 1665

"Girl with a Pearl Earring" is an oil painting by Dutch Golden Age painter Johannes Vermeer. The work is a tronie, a Dutch term for a painting of a person's head that isn't intended to be a portrait. It is renowned for its captivating gaze and the luminous quality of the light, especially on the pearl earring.
