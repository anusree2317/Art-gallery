Mona Lisa

Artist: Leonardo da Vinci

Year of Creation: c. 1503–1519

The Mona Lisa is a half-length portrait painting by Italian artist Leonardo da Vinci. It is considered an archetypal masterpiece of the Italian Renaissance and is one of the world's most famous works of art. The painting is a bust portrait of a woman, which many believe to be Lisa Gherardini. It is known for its enigmatic expression and the sitter's mysterious smile.
